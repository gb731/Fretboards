CAGED shapes on the individual chord fretboards

Dashed bands mark where each open-chord shape lands for that chord. Tap any combination of letters, such as C, A and D, to see the forms above and below each other. Notes outside the chosen shapes fade. Minor chords use the minor form (labeled like "Am shape") in the same positions. Of the minor forms, Am, Em and Dm are the ones guitarists really play. The Cm and Gm shapes are awkward and rarely used, but they are shown so the five-shape pattern stays complete. Diminished chords are left out.

Only chords switched on above appear here. Tap chords one at a time, or tap common progressions to stack them into a song, such as a verse then a chorus. Adding a progression switches on any chord it needs. Tap a step in your song to change that chord to sus2, sus4, a 7th or a 9th (or back to plain), or to remove it. Sevenths and ninths are outlined as four-string shapes. The individual fretboards then show every step in order, and Play progression plays them. Undo steps back, Repeat all doubles the song, and Clear returns to the normal order.

Synth plays a soft chord under each step, voiced to move as little as possible from chord to chord, changing chord with the guitar. Loop repeats the progression until you press Stop. While it loops you can change the tempo, drums, metronome, mix, or chord order, and the change takes effect on the next pass.

Tap any note to hear it, or tap a shape outline to hear it strummed from the low string up. Each individual chord's Play button strums one of its outlined shapes, nearest the anchor fret, so it follows your string set, zoom, and CAGED choices. If outlines are off, it picks a shape on any strings. Play progression plays your numbered steps in order, or all switched-on chords if no progression is set, with the drums and metronome (if switched on) keeping time at the same tempo and time signature. On iPad, make sure the silent switch is off.

Solid dots are the current chord. Dashed rings show the previous chord's shape, and notes with a dark ring are shared by both chords.
