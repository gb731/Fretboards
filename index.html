Guitar Triad Fretboards

Major-scale triads, CAGED shapes, and chord progressions in every key

Pick a key, then choose which chords to show. Each chord has its own color, and where two chords share a note the dot is split. Every fretboard below follows your selection.

Board size

Smaller sizes put the fretboards side by side so more fit on screen. Pair a small size with Zoom to a fret range to keep the notes readable.

Progression order for the individual fretboards

Only chords switched on above appear here. Tap them in the order you want, such as 1, 3, 4, 6. The individual fretboards then show those chords in that order, repeats included. Tap a step to remove it, or Clear to return to the normal order.

CAGED shapes on the individual chord fretboards

Dashed bands mark where each open-chord shape lands for that chord. Tap any combination of letters, such as C, A and D, to see the forms above and below each other. Notes outside the chosen shapes fade. Minor chords use the minor form (labeled like "Am shape") in the same positions. The vii chord is left out.
